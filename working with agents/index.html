Questions parents ask

FAQ

Is it really free?
Yes, the core card and app are free for every child on your account. An optional Family Plus tier is €2.99/month per family, not per child.
How is this different from Revolut <18?
Chores-linked allowances, age-matched money lessons, both parents free, and an Irish bank account that carries on past 18. No more turning 18 and finding, as one parent put it, an account ...he can't access.
Who's in control?
You are. You approve chores, set spending limits, freeze the card and get spending alerts.
Is the “game” part safe?
Rewards are real euro and real savings goals. There are no tokens, streak pressure or points to chase.
